      What do the stats mean?
      UFC opponent combined record
      The UFC records of everyone they've fought in the UFC, added together (someone they fought twice counts once). A higher win percentage means tougher opposition.
      Total pro MMA opponent combined record
      The same for every opponent in their whole pro career, including smaller promotions, using each opponent's full pro MMA record. A few opponents from small shows have no record anywhere; the page says how many were counted.
      Sig. strikes landed / min
      Significant strikes (the real, scoring ones) landed per minute of fighting.
      Striking accuracy
      Percentage of significant strikes thrown that landed.
      Sig. strikes absorbed / min
      Significant strikes taken from the opponent per minute. Lower is better.
      Striking defence
      Percentage of the opponent's significant strikes that missed.
      Takedown avg
      Takedowns landed per 15 minutes (a 3-round fight). Not a percentage.
      Takedown accuracy
      Percentage of takedown attempts that worked.
      Takedown defence
      Percentage of the opponent's takedown attempts that were stopped.
      Submission avg
      Submission attempts per 15 minutes. Not a percentage.
      Knockdown avg
      Times they knocked the opponent down, per 15 minutes.
      Control time
      How long they held the opponent in a dominant position (mostly on the ground or against the fence), per 15 minutes of fighting.

      Career numbers on this site are worked out from the fighter's UFC fights. Detailed strike and takedown stats are only recorded for UFC fights, so fights in other organisations show the result only.

      Each fighter's country is checked against where they were born, their nationality and where they're based. When those agree with each other but not with the country listed for them, the country is corrected; when nothing settles it, the listed country is kept and marked "not confirmed" (many fighters represent a country other than the one they were born in).
